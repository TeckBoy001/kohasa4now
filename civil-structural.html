Service Line — Civil & Structural Engineering

Civil &
Structural Works

Equipment foundations, structural steel fabrication, civil works, facilities construction, and access infrastructure — the structural backbone that supports oil and gas operations.

Civil & Structural Engineering Capability

Built on Strong Foundations

Our civil and structural capability covers the full range of infrastructure works that support oil and gas production facilities. From equipment pads and foundations to pipe racks, access roads, and covered facilities, we deliver durable civil works that meet client specifications and environmental requirements.

Our structural steel fabrication capability at our Elelenwo yard enables pre-fabrication of complex structural assemblies prior to site installation, improving quality and reducing site time.

Scope

Civil & Structural Engineering Services

01

Equipment Foundations

Reinforced concrete foundations for pressure vessels, heat exchangers, pumps, compressors, and fired equipment. Design review and as-built documentation.

02

Pipe Rack Structures

Structural steel pipe rack fabrication and installation. Carbon steel and galvanised steel. Designed and fabricated to client specifications at our yard.

03

Access Roads & Drainage

Laterite access roads, culverts, drainage channels, and site grading for oil and gas facilities. Environmental compliance maintained.

04

Covered Facilities

Workshop buildings, warehouse structures, generator sheds, guard houses, and security towers. Steel frame construction with appropriate cladding.

05

Security Towers & Perimeter

Observation towers, perimeter fencing, and access control structures for facility security compliance.

06

Civil Works for EPCI Projects

Integrated civil works package on EPCI contracts — civil works, foundations, drainage, and structural installations delivered alongside mechanical and piping scopes.
