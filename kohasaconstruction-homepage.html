Technical Capabilities

Construction Capability in Detail

Every scope executed to international codes and client specifications, with full documentation and as-built records. Materials: Carbon Steel · Stainless Steel · Duplex SS · CuNi · GRE/GRP. Welding: SMAW, GTAW, FCAW, SAW.

01

Interconnecting Piping

Shop and field fabrication in carbon steel, stainless steel, and duplex materials. Piece-mark system. Multiple welding processes. ASME-compliant.

02

Wellhead Platforms & Jackets

Fabrication of wellhead platforms, jackets, dolphins, piles, and structural appurtenances for onshore and offshore. Manifold installation included.

03

Storage Tank Construction

Above-ground storage tanks to API 650 and API 620. New construction and rehabilitation. Condensate, firewater, LPG, crude, and base oil tanks.

04

Pipeline Construction

Full-cycle execution: survey, staking, clearing, stringing, bending, welding, coating, trenching, lowering-in, tie-ins, pressure testing — onshore, swamp, offshore.

05

NDT & Testing Services

Hydrostatic and pneumatic pressure testing. UT, RT, Dye Penetrant (DPT), Magnetic Particle Inspection (MPI). ASNT Level 1 & 2 certified personnel.

06

LPG Plants & CPF

EPCI capability for LPG extraction plants including interconnecting piping, firefighting systems (firewater tanks, foam systems), and complete CPF installations.

07

Steel Structural Fabrication

Pipe racks, tank farms, access platforms, deck extensions, pig launchers and receivers. Pre-fabrication at yard followed by site installation.

08

Metering & Instrumentation

Procurement, fabrication, and installation of orifice assemblies, auto samplers, custody transfer meters, transmitters, and associated tubing systems.

09

Civil Works & Foundations

Equipment foundations, pipe rack supports, access roads, covered warehouses, generator sheds, security towers, and reinforced concrete culverts.
